## Summary
- What is wanted: the QA specialist in two modes, always launched as the
  `qa-specialist` agent on `agent-workflow/qa:max` by its explicit ref. The
  quality pass runs configured suites against a named commit and summarizes
  each with provenance. The bug scan examines a diff for defect families
  and emits shared finding records. Both report; neither edits nor accepts.
  Cheap first-pass review remains available on the verifier role and is not
  silently renamed QA.
- Why it matters: QA and card verification have different jobs. Giving QA
  its own max-pinned role keeps the ordinary defect hunt independent without
  turning it into either a second verifier or the acceptance verdict.

## Context
- Dispatch (pinned): every `/qa` child, `/qa --bug-scan` included, is one QA
  role launch at max by ref, never `issue-auto`, never a lower effort, and
  never the acceptance reviewer. Card verification, `/verify`,
  `/synchronize` and cheap first-pass review stay on `verifier`.
- Finding record (pinned schema, reused by B3.2): `id` (stable), `class`,
  `file:line`, `confidence`, `evidence` (verbatim snippet plus what was
  independently re-verified), and `verdict` from `CONFIRMED | REFUTED |
  INDETERMINATE`. A malformed or incomplete record is rejected.
- Fixture defect families (verbatim): `off-by-one`, `silent-catch`,
  `stale-cache-read`, `unguarded-external-call`.
- Found-nothing is a recorded result. Every scan names its scope and any
  skipped surface with the reason; an unreported skip is a COVERAGE_GAP,
  not a clean scan.
- Guidance: "A Million Tests Is Still a Survey" (survey honesty); "The Card
  Is the Contract" (reviewing in separate lanes).

## Failing test
- Test file / class: `tests/test_qa_specialist.py::TestRoleAndReportNotRepair`
- Guarantee it asserts: (a) every QA/bug-scan launch uses the QA ref at max
  and no generic issue route, while cheap review remains on verifier; (b) a
  fixture diff containing one defect of each family yields four complete
  records; (c) a clean diff yields found-nothing and a partial scan yields
  COVERAGE_GAP with the skipped surface named; (d) the scanner changes no
  file (tree hashes before/after match) and cannot emit ACCEPTED; (e) a
  record missing any schema field is rejected.
- Command: `python3 -m pytest tests/test_qa_specialist.py -q`
- exact failure on the base commit (before `scripts/qa-scan.py` exists):
  exit 1, `FileNotFoundError` / `ModuleNotFoundError` (`scripts/qa-scan.py`).

## Mutants
- M1 (self-repair): the scanner rewrites the flagged line.
  `TestRoleAndReportNotRepair::test_tree_unchanged` must turn red.
- M2 (hidden empty): a clean diff prints nothing, or a skipped surface is
  omitted instead of reported. `test_empty_and_skips_are_recorded` must turn red.
- M3 (schema drift): records omit `confidence`.
  `test_schema_rejects_incomplete` must turn red.
- M4 (family blind spot): one fixture family produces no record.
  `test_all_families_reported` must turn red.
- M5 (role collapse): QA launches through verifier below max or emits the
  acceptance verdict. `test_qa_role_and_no_acceptance` must turn red.

## Doctrine
- Principles engaged: QA is not verification and not acceptance; findings
  are reported, never repaired; found-nothing and skipped scope are results.
- Why this validation is sufficient: self-repair, hidden empty/skip, malformed
  records, blind spots and role collapse each have a killing mutant.

## DESIGN SELF-CHECK
1. Claims and pins: role, schema, scan and coverage claims map to (a)-(e);
   no claim makes QA the cheap review's name or the final verdict.
2. Constants: QA ref, effort, finding fields and verdict values come from
   the registry/schema; tests import them rather than re-type.
3. Shared state and test order: every fixture tree is hashed independently;
   scan order is not relied upon for finding ids or results.
4. Inherited environment: scanner fixtures run with clean environments and
   explicit role bindings; no provider or git state leaks between cases.
5. Mutant kills: M1-M5 name their tests; M4 also has a four-family control
   proving the fixtures exercised every required class.
6. Composition: B2.1/B2.3 own dispatch; this card owns QA behavior; B3.2
   and B3.3 reuse only the shared record schema, not a second QA role.
