## Summary
- What is wanted: two artifacts. The investigate runner takes one concrete
  proposition, drives an adversarial pass against code, tests and history,
  and returns a verdict with every claim as a finding record. The `judge`
  adjudicator is a read-only role in a separate context, invoked only when
  investigation returns INDETERMINATE or ESCALATED, or when a contradiction
  or high-stakes partial verdict remains. `advisor` may give a second
  opinion on a design or improvement but is never the judge or a verdict
  authority.
- Why it matters: contested correctness must not be settled by asking the
  author, and disagreement alone must not turn adjudication into a standing
  tax. The evidence triggers the judge; the coordinator still owns the final
  workflow decision.

## Context
- Propositions are concrete and falsifiable ("X holds on every path through
  Y"), never "look into X".
- Investigation verdicts (pinned): `CONFIRMED`, `REFUTED`, `INDETERMINATE`,
  `ESCALATED`. REFUTED carries a counterexample record. A contradiction or
  high-stakes partial verdict is an escalation trigger, not something the
  coordinator may narrate away.
- Judge rulings (pinned): `UPHOLD`, `REOPEN`, `EXPAND_SCOPE`,
  `NEEDS_HUMAN`. UPHOLD: the coordinator's decision stands. REOPEN: narrow
  the proposition and investigate again. EXPAND_SCOPE: widen the evidence
  and re-run. NEEDS_HUMAN: stop and ask one question.
- Escalation gate (pinned): `adjudicate.py` refuses unless its packet names
  an INDETERMINATE/ESCALATED verdict or a contradiction/high-stakes partial.
  A coordinator/`advisor` disagreement BY ITSELF is not a trigger, and
  advisor agreement is not a verdict. Judge is never a default final stage.
- Packet (pinned): proposition, verdict artifact, disputed claims and
  decisive file:line evidence, kept small. Missing decisive evidence is a
  refusal, not an invitation to invent a stronger property.
- Guidance: "Never Ask a Model If It's Right" (the doubt ladder and role
  split); "The Card Is the Contract" (advisor optional, judge on evidence).
